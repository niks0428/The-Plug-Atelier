Refund Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Summary: All products are digital and delivered instantly. Because access is granted immediately upon purchase, we are unable to offer refunds once a product has been delivered. Please read this policy in full before purchasing.

1. Digital Products — No Refund Policy

All products sold by The Plug Atelier are digital goods (supplier contact information, guides, and related digital content). Under the UK Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013, the right to cancel does not apply to digital content that has been downloaded or accessed, where you have given prior consent and acknowledged that the right to cancel is waived upon delivery.

By completing a purchase, you confirm that you:

  • Understand the product is a digital download delivered instantly.
  • Consent to immediate delivery of the digital content.
  • Acknowledge that your right to cancel under the Consumer Contracts Regulations is waived upon delivery.

2. Exceptions

We will issue a full refund in the following circumstances:

  • Non-delivery: You did not receive your product within 24 hours of purchase and our support team is unable to resend it.
  • Wrong product delivered: You received content for a different product than the one you purchased.
  • Duplicate charge: You were charged more than once for the same order.

To request a refund under these exceptions, contact us at admin@theplugatelier.com within 7 days of purchase, including your order details.

3. Not Eligible for Refund

  • Change of mind after purchase.
  • Claims that supplier information "didn't work" — supplier availability and pricing are outside our control and may change over time.
  • Requests made more than 7 days after purchase.

4. Chargebacks

We take fraudulent chargebacks seriously. All purchases are logged with timestamps, delivery records, and IP data. If you have a genuine issue, please contact us directly before initiating a chargeback — we are happy to resolve problems quickly and fairly.

5. How to Contact Us

For any order issues, email admin@theplugatelier.com with your order number. We aim to respond within 24 hours.
