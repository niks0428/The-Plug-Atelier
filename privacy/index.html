Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

The Plug Atelier ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights under UK GDPR.

1. Who We Are

The Plug Atelier is an online retailer of digital supplier information, operating at theplugatelier.com. For data enquiries, contact us at theplugatelier@gmail.com.

2. What Data We Collect

  • Purchase data: Your email address and payment details when you buy a product. Payment processing is handled by Stripe — we never see or store your card details.
  • Email sign-ups: If you submit your email via our "Get Supplier Updates" form, we store your email address.
  • Contact form: Name and email address if you contact us through the website.
  • Usage data: Basic analytics (page views, referrer) via GitHub Pages infrastructure. No cookies are set by us.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To fulfil your order and deliver digital products to your inbox.
  • To send you updates about new suppliers and products (only if you signed up — you can unsubscribe at any time).
  • To respond to customer support enquiries.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

4. Legal Basis (UK GDPR)

  • Contract performance — processing your order.
  • Legitimate interests — fraud prevention and business communications.
  • Consent — email marketing sign-ups (you can withdraw at any time).

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share data only with:

  • Stripe — payment processing (their privacy policy applies).
  • GitHub Pages — website hosting.

6. Data Retention

Purchase records are retained for 7 years for legal and tax purposes. Email sign-up data is retained until you unsubscribe. Contact form data is deleted after 12 months.

7. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to: access your data, correct inaccurate data, request deletion, restrict processing, and object to processing. To exercise any right, email us at theplugatelier@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Cookies

We do not use tracking cookies. Our site is a static GitHub Pages site and does not set any first-party cookies.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this URL with the updated date shown above.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email theplugatelier@gmail.com.
